About

Built for runners who want more than just mileage.

Hitendra Choudhary, an avid marathoner including a Boston Marathon finisher, brings real race experience and practical endurance coaching to every athlete. At Hitfit Tribe, the goal is simple: help runners become stronger, more resilient, and more confident in their training.

Whether you are just starting your running journey or preparing for your next race, the coaching approach blends endurance training, strength work, mobility, and mindset support so you can stay consistent and keep improving without burning out.

Coaching Programs

Programs tailored to every stage of your running journey

01

Beginner Run Foundation

Build confidence, improve consistency, and learn proper technique without overwhelm through structured weekly plans and a progressive build-up.

Who it’s for
New runners or anyone returning to running after a long break.
Prerequisites
None. Able to walk briskly for 30 minutes.
02

5K to 10K Performance

Improve speed, confidence, and endurance with targeted interval, threshold, and VO2 max sessions plus race-specific planning and recovery strategies.

Who it’s for
Runners who can already complete a 5K and want to get faster or step up to 10K.
Prerequisites
Comfortably running 5K, at least 3 runs per week.
03

Half Marathon Mentorship

Smart training volume, strength work, long-run progression, fueling guidance, and pacing strategy for race day.

Who it’s for
Runners preparing for their first or next half marathon.
Prerequisites
Comfortably running 10K.
04

Full Marathon Training

A structured build-up for race-day readiness, with progressive long runs, workouts, recovery, and weekly check-ins.

Who it’s for
Experienced runners targeting a full marathon.
Prerequisites
Recent half marathon finish and a consistent weekly running base.
05

Triathlon Training

Balanced swim, bike, and run progression with brick sessions and a plan built around your recovery capacity.

Who it’s for
Athletes preparing for sprint, Olympic, or longer triathlons.
Prerequisites
Able to swim continuously and ride a bike comfortably.
